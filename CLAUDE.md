# Skittlers league websites

- Hosted on Netlify, connected to this repo: every push to `main` deploys to production.
- Only commit and push when the user asks; a push to `main` goes live.
- Workflow: develop in the session and push to GitHub only when the user says the work is finished. After each change, rebuild the clickable preview (`node dev/build-preview.mjs <scratchpad>/preview.html`) and republish it to the same Artifact so the user can try it in Claude. The preview runs the real `lib/core.mjs` API in the browser. `npm run preview` runs a local server for browser tests; sign up as `owner@skittlers.test` for the Super Admin.
- `lib/demo.mjs` builds the demo league (see Demo site below); the preview build inlines it ahead of core.
- `public/index.html` is the whole front end (`/` = account area, `/<slug>` = a league; later `<slug>.skittlers.co.uk`). `lib/core.mjs` is the API; `netlify/functions/api.mjs` wraps it with Netlify Blobs.
- Data model: accounts (`users/`, `emails/`), leagues (`leagues/`, `slugs/`), and per season `seasons/<league>/<season>` (teams, venues, fixtures, divisions, cups, transfers, committee) plus `contacts/<league>/<season>`.
- Secrets (`SESSION_SECRET`, `SUPERADMIN_EMAIL`) live in Netlify environment variables. Never commit a `.env` file.
- Captains' addresses and mobiles are stored separately ("contacts") and must only be returned to the league's secretary or the Super Admin.
- League settings (`leagues/<id>.settings`): points for win/draw/loss, players a side, balls a hand, hands each player throws, the game (rough skittles or nomination), the tie-break order, the usual start time for league games (`startTime`, default 19:45) and the colour scheme (`theme`), the page backgrounds for light and dark mode (`background` from `BACKGROUNDS`, `darkBackground` from `DARK_BACKGROUNDS`; `scheme` = worked out from the header colour), light or dark (`mode`: `auto` follows the visitor's device, or always `light` or `dark`; `LOOK_MODES`, set in `applyTheme`) and font style (`font`, one of `FONTS` in the page, loaded from Google Fonts). Each division and cup can set its own `game`; otherwise it plays the league's.
- Roles (League setup → Admins & roles, secretary only): the league secretary (owner) and Super Admin do everything. Other admins are added by email in `leagues/<id>.members` [{ email, roles, added }] with any of `results` (Fixtures & results, cup results, captains' results, photo reading), `fixtures` (fixtures & cups secretary: games, dates, venues, divisions, cups and draws, plus results) and `media` (News & photos, report drafts, Downloads); they sign up or log in with that email and are emailed when added. `accessOf` in core works out each person's access and every route and every PATCH data change is checked against it (`ROLE_FIELDS`: a results secretary changes only results, scorecards, notes and publishing of games and cups already there); the page (`setAccess`, `can`, `TAB_NEEDS`, `body.can-*` classes, `data-need`) shows only the tabs and controls that fit. Captains' contacts go only to the secretary, the Super Admin and roles ticked in `contactRoles` (off by default; shown to them on Fixtures & results). Captain logins (`captainLogins`, off by default): `leagues/<id>.captains` [{ email, team (name) }]; a captain sees My team (add or remove players, the only change allowed) and sends their own team's results without the code. The subscription that counts is always the secretary's. Subscriptions are yearly, set by the Super Admin until payments are added.
- Cups (Manage cups page): knockout tournaments, team or individual player, full tournament or straight final. Rounds and byes are worked out from the number of entries; only the draw and results are stored, and later rounds are derived from them. Each knockout cup also has `drawMode` (`auto`: random draw, winners follow the bracket; `manual`: the secretary sets round 1 by hand into the bracket's places (`pairsForm`, Fill in at random) and each later round's matches once the round before is finished, kept in `cup.pairings[round]` = [[home, away|null]…] (`cupEntering`, `cupRoundPairs`; a round of the last two pairs itself; Change the draw until a result is in)), `semiVenue` (`home` or `neutral`, chosen later per match) and `finalAt` ({ mode: `home`|`neutral`|`set`, venue, alley }; Use this final venue for every cup copies it to all cups, straight finals included). `cupVenue` applies them; `cupMatches` in core mirrors all of this. Adding a season (Seasons page, Bring across from an earlier season) can copy venues, teams, players, divisions and cups (`copySeason` in core, `copy.cups`): a cup keeps its name, type, game, format, structure, pairs/captains and number of entries, with no entrants, draw, dates, results, checks or publishing.
- Public Cups page (`VIEWS.cup`): a knockout cup's draw shows as a Chart (`bracketHtml`) or a List (`cupListHtml`: each round with its date, its games in the same centred `.fx` boxes as the league fixtures, with pins, venue and tie notes, then byes); the choice is `S.ui.cupView`, remembered in the browser (`skittlers-cup-view`).
- Add-ons: the Super Admin sets each one (photo reading, batch photo reading, past results import, match night reports, own domain) as included in the basic subscription or an extra given per account (`site/settings` and `users/<id>.photos|.reports`).
- Photo reading: the secretary photographs the board and Claude fills in the scorecard for checking. The Super Admin sets `users/<id>.photos {until, monthlyLimit}`; readings are counted in `usage/<league>/<yyyy-mm>`. The API stores a job in `board-jobs/` and `netlify/functions/read-board-background.mjs` reads it with the Claude API (`lib/claude-reader.mjs`; needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, optional `BOARD_MODEL`/`BOARD_EFFORT`). The preview reads photos through the Claude app instead. `BOARD_FAKE=1 npm run preview` makes up readings for local tests.
- Reading a batch of photos (Fixtures & results, results role; its own add-on, `batch` in `ADDONS` (plain: on or off, `users/<id>.batch`), checked by the read-board route for `kind: "sheet"`, and it needs Photo reading too: each photo is one reading): chalkboards from a match night, or pages from a team's results book (date, Played at, Versus, that team's players, hand totals, Up/Down row, result) for old seasons. The page sends `match.kind: "sheet"` (`sheetCandidates`: the season's teams with players and venues, and the games on the chosen night or every game) to the same read-board route; `sheetMatch`/`sheetPrompt`/`SHEET_SCHEMA`/`parseSheet` in core have Claude find the game and read each side, and for a results-book page work out the other side's hand totals from Up/Down. Readings wait in a checking list kept in the browser (`BATCH`, localStorage per league and season) with `batchPlan` checks; Accept saves cards, pins and, for a side known only as totals, `fixture.hands.{home|away}` (used for hand points by `sideHands`), ticked as checked; Check and edit opens the scorecard with either side allowed empty (`optional`). Names not on a team's list are saved as written.
- Header logo animation (`bowlLogo`, on every page): each time a page opens (`route` calls `bowlSoon`), and when the logo is tapped, a small ball rolls in from the left, knocks the logo's nine pins down, fades, and the pins set back up. Nothing moves for reduced motion.
- League tables rewind (`VIEWS.tables`: little ‹ Previous · Current · Next › links above each table's team column; the date shows instead of Current on an earlier table and goes back to the current one): the tables as they stood after any match night with a league result (`standings` with a date filter); rows slide to their new places with ▲/▼ and changed points flash. Nothing moves for reduced motion.
- Positions chart (League tables, per division, Table / Positions chart switch kept in `S.ui.tableViews`): `positionsChart` draws each team's position after every match night the division played (up to the night being viewed) as a coloured line (`CHART_COLOURS`, monotone curves), as deep as the table (`PC_ROW` a place) and always the width of the page (`buildChart`, redrawn on resize: nights squeeze together, long names are shortened, crowded charts label every few dates and use smaller dots, and on phones the names only go on the right), with each team's name beside its first position on the left and, once the lines are drawn, beside its current position on the right (no separate key); tapping a name picks out its line. `drawChart` draws the lines from the first night to the latest when that division's Chart button is pressed (`chartToAnimate`: only that one; other charts on the page are drawn without animating), then fades in the right-hand names (still for reduced motion).
- Home page (`#home`, the default public page): division tables side by side, the next match night, news and photos. News posts are stored per league in `news/<league>`; photos in `media-index/<league>` with each image (and a `/thumb` copy) at `media/<league>/<id>`, served as images by `GET /api/public/<slug>/media/<id>`. Admins manage both on the News & photos page; photos linked to a cup also show on that cup's page.
- Publishing results: results, scorecards and notes reach the public only when published. Each fixture keeps a working copy plus `pub` (what was last published), a private `note` with a `notePublic` tick, and a `ready` ("checked") tick; Fixtures & results groups games by night with a Publish now button. Cups work the same way: each cup has `check` (per match id, or `final`: note, notePublic, ready) and `pub` ({ results, final, notes }); Manage cups has Publish now per round and Publish final. The public season endpoint (`publicSeason` in core) serves only what's in `pub`. Fixtures and cups without `pub` predate this and are shown as they are.
- Match night reports: when a recent match night's results are all published (or a cup night's last result is saved), the page works out the night's facts (`nightFacts`) and the API has Claude write a report through the same background function. It is saved as a draft news post for the secretary to check and publish; drafts are never public.
- Calendar feed: `GET /api/public/<slug>/calendar.ics` (optional `?team=`, `?division=`, `?season=`) serves the season's dated league games, cup matches and cup finals as iCalendar from published data only (`seasonCalendar` in core). Cup rounds come from `cupMatches` in core, which must stay in step with `cupBracket` in the page. League fixtures has an Add to calendar button (Google, Apple, copy link).
- Player transfers (Manage teams, players & roles): moving a player records `transfers[id] {date, first, last, nick, key, from, to}` in the season and moves them between teams' player lists. Stats follow a player's games for the old team (up to the transfer date) to the new team via `transferKey`; each game keeps the team they played for. The latest transfer of a player can be undone.
- Results from captains: with a captains' code set in League setup (`leagues/<id>.captainCode`, never public; `takesResults` is), the public Send a result tab lets captains pick the date, their team and fill in the scorecard. `POST /api/public/<slug>/seasons/<sid>/submissions` checks the code and card (`cleanCard`), stores it in `submissions/<league>/<season>` and emails the league owner through Resend when `RESEND_API_KEY` and `MAIL_FROM` are set. Admins see a count on the Fixtures & results tab and approve (fills the fixture, ticked as checked), approve and publish, or reject.
- Committee (Manage teams, players & roles): President, Chairman, Treasurer and Secretary (one each, stored under those ids) and any number of committee members, each a player picked from the season's teams, in the season's `committee`. Shown on the home page under Meet your committee with the team they play for now (`committeeTeam` follows transfers).
- Colour schemes (League setup → Look of your site): `THEMES` in the page (between `THEMES-START`/`THEMES-END`) are a header and highlight colour each; `themeTokens` works out every colour for light and dark mode and shades text until it reaches 4.5 to 1. `dev/themes.test.mjs` (part of `npm test`) checks every scheme's text pairs.
- Help & requests: each account has one conversation with the Super Admin in `support/<userId>` (messages, unread counts both ways). Secretaries use the Help & requests page (also linked from the league header); the Super Admin replies under Messages. Pages check for new messages every 20 seconds while open and the counts every minute. New messages are emailed to `SUPERADMIN_EMAIL`, replies to the secretary, through Resend when it's set up.
- Sharing news: each published post has its own address `/<slug>/news/<id>` with Share buttons (phone share, Facebook, WhatsApp, X, copy link). `netlify/functions/share.mjs` serves that address as the app with the post's preview tags (`newsSharePage` in core: title, opening text, first photo); the page then opens the post at `#news/<id>`. Asset paths in the page are absolute (`/vendor/...`) so they load from that deeper address.
- Downloads: admins add files on the Downloads admin page (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text, JPEG, PNG; up to 4 MB, 60 per league; the first bytes are checked against the kind). Index in `docs-index/<league>`, each file at `docs/<league>/<id>`; served by `GET /api/public/<slug>/docs/<id>` (PDFs and pictures open in the browser, `?download=1` or any other kind downloads). The public Downloads tab shows once there's a file.
- Importing past seasons (Seasons page; the `history` add-on, Past results import: the page sends `import: true` on the season POST, PUT and data PATCH it makes, and core's `importGate` refuses them with 402 without the add-on): one CSV per season, one row per game (Date, Home Team, Away Team, Home Pins, Away Pins; optional Competition, Division, Round, Venue; template in `public/templates/results-template.csv`). `resultsImportPlan` checks the rows; importing creates the season, adds its teams, divisions and venues to that season only, and saves the games as plain fixtures (cup games as `comp: "cup"` with the cup's name in `round`), live as they are. A game not played is marked Home Walkover or Away Walkover (in the pins columns, or anywhere in the row; "Walkover" or "W/O" alone in one side's pins column also works) and saved as `walkover: "home"|"away"` with no pins: the tables give that side the win (P, W/L, win points, no pins), results show W/O (`walkover`, `decided`, `winnerSide` in the page; `pub.walkover` when published). A final where the highest pins wins (singles or pairs) is one row per entry with no Away Team and Final in Round; it becomes a straight-final cup (`importedFinalCup`, entrants by name, no teams added). The public site stays on its current season. Import into can instead add a file to a season already set up (`importInto`: league games, then each cup, a file at a time): teams (also by old names), divisions and venues are matched by name and only new ones added, games already there (same date, teams, competition and round) are left out, and the season's dates stretch to take in the games. After an import (or from a season's Check names button) `openNameCheck` lists that season's teams and venues against the reference season (the public one, or the newest other), suggesting close matches (`nameLikeness`): Same as (renames in that season, joining duplicates and their games; venues also take the address and alleys), Old name of (adds to `teamNames`), or New.
- Team logos (Manage teams: Add logo / Change logo / Remove logo on each team, secretary only): `PUT /api/leagues/<id>/logos {team, image|null}` keeps one image per team for the league (`logos/<league>/<id>`, at most 160 px, PNG so see-through backgrounds stay; `leagues/<id>.logos` = { lower-case team name: id }, public), served by `GET /api/public/<slug>/logos/<id>`. `logoFor` finds a team's logo by its name or old names (`teamKey`), so it follows the team across seasons; `linkTeamNames` puts it small before team names on the public pages, the Teams page shows it larger (`logoImg`), loaded by `loadLogos` (from memory in the preview).
- Previous team names: `leagues/<id>.teamNames` = [{ name (now), was: [older names] }], set on the team form (renaming a team keeps its old name automatically) and public. `teamKey` matches a team across seasons by any of its names: past meetings, and the season-by-season record on the public Teams page (`teamHistory`, which works out each season's table with `withSeason`).
- Team pages: any team name (`.tn`) on the public pages is a link (`linkTeamNames`, marked by a MutationObserver; `teamByName` matches old names through `teamKey`) to `#team/<id>`, the Teams page (teams in alphabetical order; a click anywhere on a team's box, other than a link or button, opens or closes it) showing just that team, open, with its League honours (finished seasons from `teamHistory`: division winners, runners-up, and the wooden spoon for last) and Cup honours, each shown only when there is one (`teamHonours`: cups won or runners-up in every season, from `seasonCupResults`, which Past seasons uses too) and season by season record. Names of teams no longer in the league aren't links.
- Own domain (add-on, connected by hand for now): the secretary types the league's address in League setup (`leagues/<id>.domain`, no www; `domains/<host>` points back to the league) and is shown the DNS records (A @ 75.2.60.5, CNAME www skittlers.netlify.app). The Super Admin adds it as a domain alias in Netlify, then marks it connected under Super Admin → Own domains (`domainConnected`). On a league's own host the page looks up its league with `GET /api/public-domain/<host>` and shows it at "/"; account links go to `MAIN_ORIGIN`.
- Site guides: "What it does" (`#guide`, public, linked from the front page) lists what a league secretary, the other roles and visitors get, then the subscription price breaks and extras live from `GET /api/site`; the Super Admin's Site guide (`#super-guide`, Super Admin only) adds the Super Admin section, the public link to copy and a link to edit prices. Both come from `guideHtml` (descriptions of extras in `GUIDE_EXTRAS`); keep their lists up to date when features change.
- Front page (`#welcome`, what visitors to the main address see when not logged in): features, screenshots (`public/img/shot-*.jpg`, made from an example league; the preview carries them inside the page), photo reading, setting up, prices, sign up and a contact form. Prices are in `site/settings.plans`, the price breaks (any number, each `{ maxTeams, price }`; default up to 30, 60 and 90 teams; 0 shows "Ask us"; bigger leagues are on application), set in Super Admin, and served with the extras by `GET /api/site`. `POST /api/contact` (with a hidden box that catches robots, and at most 5 an hour per email) puts the message in the Super Admin's Messages as a guest conversation; replies to guests go by email.
- League plans: each league has `plan { maxTeams (null when on application), price, since }`, starting on the smallest break. A save that takes a season still being played over the plan's teams (season PUT, data PATCH, a new season copied from another) is refused with 402 `planNeeded` (`planOffer`: the new break and the extra for the rest of the owner's subscription year, by days); the page asks "Do you wish to proceed?" (`planDialog`) and resends with `acceptPlan: true`, which moves the league up and adds an entry to `league.planChanges` for the Super Admin to bill (Super Admin → Price break moves to bill, with Mark as billed). Past seasons and the Super Admin aren't limited.
- Demo site (`/demo`, linked from the front page as See the demo site): Westcombe & District Skittles League, made up by `lib/demo.mjs` (`buildDemo`, seeded so it's the same every time): 30 teams with logos (SVG) and 8 players each in 3 divisions, two finished seasons (2024–25 results only, then promotion and relegation into 2025–26 with every scorecard), four team knockout cups (one drawn by hand, neutral semis, finals at a set alley), the Captains Cup, Pairs Cup and Sticker Up Cup finals, a committee and news. Core's `withDemo` lays it over the store (read-only, left out of listings, `demo` slug reserved) so the real public API and page serve it; the league routes refuse it, so nobody, not even the Super Admin, can manage it. The page shows a Demo site bar above the header (`renderDemo`; × goes back to the front page) with Try colours & fonts (scheme, backgrounds, light/dark, font; kept in sessionStorage `skittlers-demo-look` for that visit, never saved). Keep the demo up to date: when a feature is added, give `lib/demo.mjs` something that shows it.
- Ideas for later (the user wants these kept in mind, not built yet): card payments for subscriptions, price-break moves and extras (for example Stripe); connecting own domains automatically through the Netlify API; a second CSV import for past seasons' player scores; captains using photo reading; captains sending results for knockout cup rounds.
- Past seasons tab (`#past`, shown when there's a season other than the public one): pick a season to see its final league tables side by side in division name order (Division 1, 2, …) with the champions, then each cup (knockout, straight final or plain imported cup games) with its winner and runner-up; a cup's full results open when it's clicked. Imported cup games without a Round are put in rounds by night (`plainCupRounds`: named Final, Semi-finals, Quarter-finals from the end while each round has half the games of the one before, Round 1, 2… before that), and a game against a team called "Bye" shows as a bye. Above the tables: ‹ Previous · Final tables · Next › steps through that season night by night (rows slide; "Leaders after …" instead of Champions part way through), and each division has its own Table / Chart switch (`S.ui.pastViews`; a charted division takes the full width in its place, the others stay side by side) showing its chart for the season (`positionsChart` with `S.chartSeason` set, so `CHART_DATA` keeps that season's team names; `drawNewCharts`, `pickChartTeam` shared with League tables). Built with `withSeason` on `seasonFor` data. There's no season dropdown in the header any more: visitors always see the public season, and admins choose the season they work on from the Seasons page.
